## Company overview

NOORLUME (Energy & Engineering) is a renewable energy and MEP design
consultancy. It provides professional engineering solutions for solar PV
plants, electrical systems, mechanical systems and building services.

Tagline: Illuminating Innovation. Engineering Tomorrow.

Positioning: NOORLUME delivers integrated renewable energy and MEP engineering
solutions through technical excellence, intelligent design and sustainable
innovation.

NOORLUME is an engineering consultancy. It is not a solar panel installer or
equipment supplier.

Regions served: clients in India, the Middle East, GCC countries and
international markets.

Vision: To become a trusted engineering consultancy delivering innovative and
sustainable solutions for the energy and built environment sectors.

Mission: To deliver technically sound, efficient and sustainable engineering
solutions through innovation, precision and professional excellence.

## How an engagement works

1. Enquiry: the visitor shares basic project details (location, type,
   capacity or building size, stage, required service, timeline).
2. Scoping: the NOORLUME team reviews the requirement and may ask for inputs
   such as site details, drawings or specifications.
3. Proposal: the team issues a scope of work, deliverables list and
   commercial proposal.
4. Engineering: work proceeds against an agreed deliverables list with
   review stages.

Fees, turnaround times and commercial terms depend on the project scope and
are confirmed only by the NOORLUME team in a written proposal. The assistant
must not quote prices or delivery dates.

## Useful information to request for a solar PV enquiry

Site location, plant capacity (AC and DC if known), mounting type (fixed
tilt, tracker, rooftop, carport), grid connection voltage, project stage
(concept, tender, detailed design, construction, operating), and the services
required.

## Useful information to request for an MEP enquiry

Building type and location, approximate built-up area and number of floors,
project stage, disciplines required (mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire
protection, ELV), and available architectural drawings.
