## Content rhythm

Two to three posts per week. Technical content performs best when it is specific.

| Week | Post 1 | Post 2 | Post 3 (optional) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Launch announcement with brand story | Document carousel (PDF): "What solar PV engineering covers" | Founder's personal post sharing the launch |
| 2 | Article link: DC cable sizing, the three checks | Engineering tip (text only): Isc versus Imp | MEP carousel: four disciplines |
| 3 | Article link: Performance ratio | Short video: reel 7 (cable sizing) reformatted 1:1 | Poll: biggest cause of site queries |
| 4 | Article link: Inverter selection checks | Approach post: design basis first | Enquiry post: "Planning a project?" |

## Practices

- Publish knowledge-centre articles on the website first, then post a summary with the link.
- PDF document carousels outperform image posts for technical explainers; reuse the Instagram carousel templates at 1080 × 1350.
- The founder's and engineers' personal profiles carry further than the company page. Share company posts with a line of personal commentary.
- Tag standards bodies or software only when genuinely relevant, and never tag clients without permission.
- Add the company page link to the website footer by setting `NEXT_PUBLIC_LINKEDIN_URL`.
